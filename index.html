About the club

Practise speaking. Grow as a leader.

OneWorld Toastmasters Club is part of Toastmasters International. Members work through the Pathways learning programme at their own pace, giving prepared speeches, speaking off the cuff and taking on meeting roles that build leadership.

Prepared speeches

Pathways projects, 4–7 minutes each.

Table Topics

Impromptu answers in 1–2 minutes.

Evaluations

Kind, specific feedback for every speaker.
